## ✨ Key Features

<details>
<summary>👤 <b>Customer Features</b></summary>

- 🔑 **Secure Authentication:** Easy sign-up and login with Email or Google.
- 📝 **Profile Management:** Save measurements, favorite designs, and view order history.
- 🔖 **Bookmark Tailors:** Save your favorite tailors for quick access.
- 🔍 **Advanced Search:** Browse and filter tailors by price, rating, and location.
- 📦 **Custom Orders:** Easily place new orders for stitching or alterations with all necessary details.
- 🔔 **Real-Time Notifications:** Track your order status from pickup to delivery.
- 💬 **In-App Chat:** Communicate directly with your tailor for any queries.

</details>

<details>
<summary>✂️ <b>Tailor Features</b></summary>

- 🛠️ **Profile Customization:** Create a professional profile to showcase your skills and portfolio.
- 📈 **Order Dashboard:** A simple interface to accept, manage, and update order statuses.
- 💰 **Service & Pricing:** Define your own services and set custom pricing.
- 📊 **Earnings Overview:** Track your earnings and manage withdrawal requests.
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews:** Build credibility with genuine customer feedback.

</details>

<details>
<summary>⚙️ <b>Admin Panel</b></summary>

- ✅ **Verification System:** Approve or reject new tailor registrations to maintain quality.
- 📈 **Platform Analytics:** Monitor key metrics like revenue, user activity, and platform performance.

</details>
